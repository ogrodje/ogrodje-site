# Politika zasebnosti

Velja od 19. oktobra 2026.

Ta politika opisuje, katere osebne podatke Ogrodje obdeluje na spletni strani [ogrodje.si](https://ogrodje.si), v
**MetaOS** ([metaos.ogrodje.si](https://metaos.ogrodje.si)), našem orodju za pripravo in upravljanje epizod, ki ga
uporablja ekipa Ogrodja, in v e-pošti Ogrodja.

## Spletna stran ogrodje.si

- Ne uporabljamo analitike, oglasov ali piškotkov za sledenje.
- V vašem brskalniku shranimo le izbrano temo (svetla ali temna). Ta podatek ne zapusti vaše naprave.
- Stran gostuje pri ponudniku Netlify, ki kot vsak spletni strežnik beleži tehnične podatke o zahtevah (na primer IP
  naslov in vrsto brskalnika). Slike se nalagajo iz omrežja Hygraph, iskalnik pa vaš iskalni niz pošlje na naš iskalni
  strežnik.

## MetaOS

V MetaOS se prijavite prek našega strežnika za prijavo ([metaos-auth.ogrodje.si](https://metaos-auth.ogrodje.si)), z
Google ali Discord računom.

- **Kaj prejmemo.** Ob prijavi z Google računom prejmemo vaše ime, e-poštni naslov in identifikator Google računa. Ob
  prijavi z Discord računom prejmemo uporabniško ime, prikazno ime, e-poštni naslov in identifikator računa.
- **Zakaj.** Podatke uporabimo za prijavo, za odobritev dostopa (vsako novo prijavo ročno odobri skrbnik) in da prijavo
  po e-poštnem naslovu povežemo z osebo v naši evidenci gostov in sodelavcev.
- **Zgodovina sprememb.** Vsako spremembo v MetaOS zabeležimo skupaj s tem, kdo jo je naredil.
- **Google Drive.** Do vašega Google Drive, e-pošte ali drugih podatkov v vašem Google računu ne dostopamo. Dokumente
  Ogrodja v Google Drive MetaOS ureja prek ločenega Google računa Ogrodja, ki ima dostop le do map, ki jih z njim
  delimo.
- Uporaba podatkov, ki jih MetaOS prejme prek Google API-jev, je skladna s
  [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
  vključno z zahtevami Limited Use.

## E-pošta Ogrodja

Ogrodje si dopisuje tudi z naslova [iva.ogrodje@gmail.com](mailto:iva.ogrodje@gmail.com), Googlovega računa Ogrodja,
ki ga uporablja MetaOS.

- **Kaj obdelujemo.** Sporočila, ki jih izmenjate s tem naslovom: pošiljatelja, prejemnike, zadevo, datum, besedilo in
  imena priponk.
- **Zakaj.** Za dogovarjanje z gosti in sodelavci, na primer o snemanju epizod. MetaOS sporočila bere ter v imenu
  Ogrodja pripravlja in pošilja odgovore; pri tem lahko ekipa uporablja orodja umetne inteligence, na primer Claude.
- **Kje.** Sporočila hrani Gmail. Tudi zanje velja, da je uporaba podatkov, ki jih MetaOS prejme prek Google API-jev,
  skladna z zahtevami Limited Use.

## Komu posredujemo podatke

Osebnih podatkov ne prodajamo in jih ne uporabljamo za oglaševanje. MetaOS in strežnik za prijavo tečeta na strežnikih,
ki jih upravlja Ogrodje. Pri prijavi sodelujeta Google oziroma Discord, ki ju izberete sami, e-pošto Ogrodja hrani
Google, stran ogrodje.si pa gostuje pri Netlify.

## Hramba in vaše pravice

Podatke o računu hranimo, dokler imate dostop do MetaOS. Zgodovino sprememb hranimo kot evidenco dela na epizodah.
Sporočila hranimo, dokler jih potrebujemo za sodelovanje z vami; na zahtevo jih izbrišemo.

Imate pravico do dostopa do svojih podatkov, njihovega popravka ali izbrisa, omejitve obdelave in ugovora. Za
uveljavljanje pravic ali vprašanja nam pišite na [otobrglez@gmail.com](mailto:otobrglez@gmail.com). Pritožbo lahko
vložite tudi pri [Informacijskem pooblaščencu](https://www.ip-rs.si).

O spremembah te politike bomo obvestili na tej strani.

# Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026.

This policy describes which personal data Ogrodje processes on the website [ogrodje.si](https://ogrodje.si), in
**MetaOS** ([metaos.ogrodje.si](https://metaos.ogrodje.si)), the tool the Ogrodje team uses to prepare and manage
episodes, and in Ogrodje's email.

## The ogrodje.si website

- We don't use analytics, ads or tracking cookies.
- Your browser stores only the theme you picked (light or dark). It never leaves your device.
- The site is hosted by Netlify, which, like any web server, logs technical request data (such as IP address and
  browser type). Images load from Hygraph's network, and the search sends your query to our search server.

## MetaOS

You sign in to MetaOS through our sign-in server ([metaos-auth.ogrodje.si](https://metaos-auth.ogrodje.si)), with a
Google or Discord account.

- **What we receive.** Signing in with Google gives us your name, email address and Google account ID. Signing in with
  Discord gives us your username, display name, email address and account ID.
- **Why.** We use them to sign you in, to approve access (an admin approves every new sign-in by hand) and to link your
  sign-in to a person in our records of guests and contributors by email address.
- **Change history.** Every change in MetaOS is recorded together with who made it.
- **Google Drive.** We don't access your Google Drive, email or any other data in your Google account. MetaOS edits
  Ogrodje's documents in Google Drive through a separate Ogrodje Google account, which can only access the folders
  shared with it.
- MetaOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
  [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including
  the Limited Use requirements.

## Ogrodje's email

Ogrodje also writes from [iva.ogrodje@gmail.com](mailto:iva.ogrodje@gmail.com), Ogrodje's Google account that MetaOS
uses.

- **What we process.** The messages you exchange with that address: sender, recipients, subject, date, text and the
  names of attachments.
- **Why.** To arrange things with guests and contributors, such as recording episodes. MetaOS reads the messages and
  drafts and sends replies on Ogrodje's behalf; the team may use AI tools, such as Claude, to do so.
- **Where.** Gmail stores the messages. MetaOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Limited
  Use requirements for them too.

## Who we share data with

We don't sell personal data or use it for advertising. MetaOS and the sign-in server run on servers managed by Ogrodje.
Google or Discord, whichever you choose, take part in signing in, Google stores Ogrodje's email, and ogrodje.si is
hosted by Netlify.

## Retention and your rights

We keep account data for as long as you have access to MetaOS. The change history is kept as the record of work on
episodes. We keep messages for as long as we need them to work with you, and delete them on request.

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its processing and to object to it. To use these
rights or ask a question, write to [otobrglez@gmail.com](mailto:otobrglez@gmail.com). You can also lodge a complaint
with the Slovenian [Information Commissioner](https://www.ip-rs.si/en/).

We will announce changes to this policy on this page.
